PRIVACY.TXT

Privacy notice for andresmit.co.za. Last updated: November 2026.

Short version: my code sets no cookies, there are no accounts, and I store nothing about you. The services the site runs on see what any website sees, and they are listed below.

1. Who runs this site

André Smit, a software engineer in Cape Town, South Africa. It's a personal portfolio with no ads and no income. To reach me, use Contact Me on the home page.

2. What this site keeps about you

Nothing. The site's own code sets no cookies, has no sign-in, and doesn't save anything in your browser's storage.

3. Hosting and visit counts

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and the details your browser sends with each request, so it can deliver the pages and keep the site safe from attacks. It may set a short-lived security cookie (such as __cf_bm) to tell people from bots. That cookie is strictly necessary and isn't used to track you.

Page views are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, and Cloudflare says it doesn't collect or use visitors' personal data. I only ever see totals, such as how many people viewed a page.

4. The contact button

My email address only appears after Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person. Turnstile loads when you press Decrypt Contact Info, not before. It looks at signals from your browser and device, and my Cloudflare Worker passes your IP address to Cloudflare to verify the result. I don't store any of it.

5. Other services, only when you open something

Some windows show content from elsewhere. Nothing loads from these services until you open the part of the site that uses them, and each receives your IP address and browser details, as with any web request:

  • Spotify window: album and artist pictures from Spotify.
  • Videos folder: posters from TMDB.
  • Task Manager: live job data from GitHub's API.
  • Screensaver: the p5.js library from cdnjs, run by Cloudflare.
  • Deleting every desktop icon: a sound file from Dropbox.
  • Shutting down: a video from YouTube, which may set its own cookies.

Links to other sites, such as LinkedIn, GitHub and Strava, only reach them if you click. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

6. Why, and where

The basis for this processing is my legitimate interest in running the site, keeping it secure and knowing roughly how many people visit it (POPIA section 11(1)(f), GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Cloudflare and the other services are based outside South Africa, mostly in the United States, so this information can be processed there under their own data protection terms.

7. Your rights

You can ask what personal information I hold about you (from this site: none), and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Use Contact Me.

If you're not happy with my answer, you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator, or to the data protection authority where you live.

8. Changes

Until November 2026 this site used Google Analytics, which set cookies. It has been removed. If anything here changes, this file will be updated, and its history is public on GitHub.
